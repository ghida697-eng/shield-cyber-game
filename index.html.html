Your 5-point safety checklist

  1. Pause. Urgency is the scammer’s favorite tool.
  2. Check the sender and hover over links before tapping.
  3. Never share codes, passwords or private photos.
  4. Turn on two-factor authentication and use a password manager.
  5. Tell someone you trust and report it. You are never to blame.
